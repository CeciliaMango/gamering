Why I Want to Be a Fox

Sometimes, even when I have nothing to do, I find it difficult to enjoy my free time. I feel guilty when I am not being productive, as if every moment needs to have a purpose.

I imagine becoming a fox because a fox does not need to justify its existence through productivity. It can sleep, wander, play, and simply exist.

But what if I became a fox and still carried my human thoughts with me?
